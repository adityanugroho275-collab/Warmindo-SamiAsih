Black Bean Mushroom Soup

April 17, 2017    By Sara Johnson

Every Tuesday a big pot goes on at seven in the morning, and by noon half of our regulars have asked whether it is ready yet. It is the black bean mushroom soup, and it is the one dish we have never been allowed to take off the board.

It started as a staff lunch. John, our chef, had a bag of dried black beans, a tray of mushrooms that needed using and an afternoon with nothing booked. Nobody planned a signature dish. The pot simply emptied faster than anything else that week.

The Recipe Story

The beans soak overnight and cook slowly in vegetable stock until they give way when pressed against the side of the pot. The mushrooms go in later, browned first in olive oil so they keep some bite. Nothing is blended to a paste; we like a soup you can still find things in.

A good soup, like a good story, is made slowly and shared warmly.

Our guests tell us it tastes like something their grandmother would have made, which is the best review we know how to get.

What Goes In The Pot

  • black beans / vegetable stock
  • fresh mushrooms / olive oil
  • garlic / onion / celery
  • tomato paste / oregano
  • cream / black pepper

Serve it warm, with bread for the bowl, and share the pot with the people you like best. If you would rather eat it at home, ask at the counter and we will pack a litre for you.
